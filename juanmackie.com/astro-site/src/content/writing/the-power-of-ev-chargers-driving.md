The rise of electric vehicles (EVs) is undeniable and businesses are quickly realising the potential benefits of offering EV charging stations on their premises, at least I think so. I recently jumped on the bandwagon picking up a BYD Seal Performance *([yes 0-100 in 3.6 seconds is not required, but I have not lost a red light since picking it up](https://youtu.be/nOiqKv5Mn0w?si=HCvXO-Jhu-KS9a8v)*🙃*)*. On more than one occasion I have changed my choices based solely on a destination having an EV charger. Just last night we went for dinner at a restaurant we have never considered only due to them having Ultra Fast Chargers on site, which got me wondering is there more to this? Even we, a business with zero business where clients come to our office install a 22kw Fronius WattPilot EV charger which will be free for public use *(we feel it is the right thing to do as we have 30KW of Solar and lot us goes unused)* Not only does it attract environmentally-conscious customers, but it can also lead to increased spending and business growth and public optics. In this blog post, I explore the reasons why having an EV charger can be a game-changer for your business and how it can drive customer spending.

#### Attracting Eco-Conscious Customers

##### The Growing Market of EV Owners

As more and more people become aware of the environmental impact of traditional gas-powered vehicles, the demand for electric vehicles is on the rise. In fact, according to a report by the International Energy Agency, the number of electric cars on the road is expected to reach 125 million by 2030. This growing market of EV owners presents a unique opportunity for businesses to attract a new customer base by offering EV charging stations on their premises.

##### The Appeal of Sustainable Businesses

Customers are becoming increasingly conscious of the environmental impact of their purchasing decisions. A study by Nielsen found that 66% of global consumers are willing to pay more for products and services from companies that are committed to sustainability. By offering EV charging stations, businesses can demonstrate their commitment to sustainability and appeal to this growing market of eco-conscious consumers.

##### Building Brand Loyalty with EV Owners

EV owners are a loyal customer base. Once they find a business that offers EV charging, they are likely to return again and again. This can lead to increased customer retention and repeat business, driving long-term growth for your business.

#### Increasing Customer Spending

##### The Power of Dwell Time

When customers come to your business to charge their EVs, they are likely to spend more time on your premises than they would otherwise. This increased dwell time can lead to additional spending, as customers take the opportunity to shop, dine, or use other services while they wait for their vehicle to charge.

##### The Benefits of Convenience

Customers appreciate convenience and are willing to pay for it. By offering EV charging stations, businesses can provide a valuable service that sets them apart from their competitors. This convenience can lead to increased customer satisfaction and loyalty, driving repeat business and increasing overall spending.

##### The Impact on Impulse Purchases

The longer customers spend on your premises, the more likely they are to make impulse purchases. This can be a significant source of revenue for businesses, as impulse purchases often result in higher margins than planned purchases.

#### Enhancing Your Business's Reputation

##### Positioning Your Business as a Sustainability Leader

By offering EV charging stations, businesses can enhance their reputation as a leader in sustainability. This can attract new customers who are looking for businesses that share their values and are committed to making a positive impact on the environment.

##### The Power of Word-of-Mouth Marketing

Customers who have a positive experience at your business are likely to tell their friends and family about it. By offering EV charging stations, businesses can create a unique selling point that sets them apart from their competitors and generates positive word-of-mouth marketing.

##### Attracting Media Attention

Businesses that are committed to sustainability are often featured in local media outlets, which can generate positive publicity and attract new customers. By offering EV charging stations, businesses can position themselves as a leader in sustainability and attract media attention.

In conclusion, having EV charging stations on business premises can be a powerful tool for attracting environmentally-conscious customers, increasing customer spending, and enhancing a business's reputation as a leader in sustainability. By offering this valuable amenity, businesses can differentiate themselves from their competitors, drive long-term growth, and make a positive impact on the environment.
