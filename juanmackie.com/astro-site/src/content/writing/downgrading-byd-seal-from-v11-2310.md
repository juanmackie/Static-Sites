*This process carries the risk of bricking your car's screen. Proceed at your own risk, and understand that I am not responsible for any damage caused by this process.*

### **Requirements**

- A USB drive with at least 8GB of storage
- A computer with internet access
- Firmware file (download link provided below or click[[HERE]](https://bit.ly/3KhOL2s)
- Approximately 1 hour of your time

### Let's Get Started

##### Step 1: Download the Firmware File

Download the firmware file from the provided link or click [[HERE]](https://bit.ly/3KhOL2s)

##### Step 2: Prepare the USB Drive

##### *(It is critical you have the name and captivation of letters EXACTLY like I show)*

1. Rename the downloaded file to `UpdateFull.zip`
2. Plug in your USB drive and format it to FAT32 or exFAT.
3. Create a folder named: `BYDUpdatePackage`on the USB drive.
4. Inside the `BYDUpdatePackage` folder, create another folder named `msm8953_64`.
5. Copy the `UpdateFull.zip` file into the `msm8953_64` folder.

##### The USB

**File Structure should look like the below**

Ensure the file structure looks like this:

- `BYDUpdatePackage``msm8953_64``UpdateFull.zip`

## **Downgrading**

Now that we have the USB drive ready, we can start the downgrade process.Go in your car and plug in the USB drive in the USB port.

##### Step 1: Plug in the USB Drive 💾

Insert the USB drive into the USB port in your car. Make sure it's securely connected and ready for the next steps.

##### Step 2: Confirm the Downgrade ✅

Click on the pop-up notification to confirm and initiate the downgrade process. The car will then begin the process of downgrading to the previous software version.

##### Step 3: wait ⏱️

Once you've confirmed the downgrade, the process will begin. Please be patient and allow the car to complete the downgrade, which may take approximately 30 minutes to 1 hour to finish.

You have to wait for the screen to reach 100%. If completed correctly, you will see the T&C’s which need to be accepted.
