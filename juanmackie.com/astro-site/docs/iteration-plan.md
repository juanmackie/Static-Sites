# 30 / 60 / 90-day iteration plan

## 30 days

- Fix broken links, navigation friction, mobile layout issues, and third-party failures.
- Review whether visitors reach `/work` and `/thesis` from the hero.
- Review permission-dependent copy before publishing any additional fire-protection specifics.

## 60 days

- Strengthen the weakest case study using actual questions and behaviour.
- Improve the most visited route's proof, outcome language, and next-step CTA.
- Review status and `lastReviewed` fields across the project archive.

## 90 days

- Use Search Console and analytics to decide whether fire-protection content should expand into a product page, deeper case study, or other route.
- Do not add a newsletter, lead magnet, or complex experimentation without evidence of a relevant audience.
- Compare qualified email conversations and Work/Thesis engagement against the pre-launch baseline.

## Suggested events

- Hero `See the work` click
- Hero email click
- Thesis view
- Case-study view
- Project outbound click
- Writing topic click
- Archive open
- Audio interaction
- Chat open and submission count, never raw message content
- Theme switch
