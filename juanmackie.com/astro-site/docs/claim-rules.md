# Claim rules

The site distinguishes what was built from what was validated.

## Evidence ladder

1. Idea
2. Prototype
3. Working locally
4. Publicly released
5. Used by Juan
6. Used by others
7. Deployed commercially
8. Measured outcome
9. Recurring revenue

A public repository proves existence. It does not prove adoption, reliability, revenue, or customer value.

## Publishing rules

- Use `built and deployed; usage not yet measured` when adoption is unknown.
- Do not publish customer-identifying information, site details, security details, or compliance-sensitive information without permission.
- Fire-protection copy must distinguish administrative assistance, workflow automation, and decision support from certified inspection or compliance decisions.
- Do not claim safety improvements without evidence.
- Robotics is future direction, not deployed capability.
- Mark permission-dependent copy with `[CONFIRM]` until reviewed by Juan.
- `lastReviewed` dates change only after material content review.

## Current sensitive items

The public fire-protection copy keeps role, employer naming, workflow details, and metrics intentionally general. Review permission before adding specifics.
